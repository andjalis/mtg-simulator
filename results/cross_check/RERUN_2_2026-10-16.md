## Why it didn't move

The 16 matchups use 11 decks, and the new coverage barely touches them: their ignored-card counts
dropped by at most one per deck (Azorius Artifact Control 9 → 8, Dimir Control 4 → 3), and Izzet
Spellementals, Boros Dragons, Landfall and Jund are unchanged. The coverage gain (726 → 1,020 cards)
is real but sits in other cards. So the 8 remaining flags are the same ones:

- **Izzet vs Landfall (8% vs 72%), Boros Dragons vs Izzet (95% vs 57%), Dimir Control vs Jund
  (52% vs 20%):** all Grandmaster/Master decks that still ignore 3 to 6 cards each. Worth reading
  which specific cards (listed per deck in `rerun_flagged_2.json`) before touching the AI.
- **Azorius Artifact Control vs Dimir Control (37% vs 72%):** still 8 ignored cards.
- **UB Snow vs UW Flyers, Green Stompy, Land Glut (+24 to +30):** no ignored cards on either side, so
  this is the pilot gap, not card coverage. Only an AI change or a Forge-side look can move it.
