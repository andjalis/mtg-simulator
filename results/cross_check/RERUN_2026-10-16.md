## What changed

1. **Azorius Artifact Control now plays.** Its ignored cards halved (18 → 9) and it wins games:
   0–3% before, 19–80% now. Five of its seven matchups are no longer flagged, two match Forge almost
   exactly (80% vs 80%, 37% vs 40%), and only the Dimir Control matchup is still flagged (34% vs 72%).
2. **UB Snow against Grandmaster decks came down toward Forge.** Against Landfall 71% → 41%
   (Forge 25%), Dimir Midrange 71% → 55% (40%), Jund 82% → 74% (45%). Two of the three are no longer
   flagged; Jund still is.
3. **Unchanged, as expected:** UB Snow against the three fully simulated decks (Land Glut, UW Flyers,
   Green Stompy) is still about 25–30 points above Forge. No card text is ignored there, so this
   remains the pilot gap the first run inferred (Forge's AI plays control worse), not card coverage.
4. **Got worse:** Izzet Spellementals vs Mono-Green Landfall went from 22% to 8% (Forge 72%).
   Landfall's newly supported cards made it much stronger in the built-in engine while Izzet still
   ignores 5 cards. Boros Dragons vs Izzet (95% vs 57%) and Dimir Control vs Jund (55% vs 20%) are
   unchanged. These three Grandmaster/Master pairs are the next things to look at.

Forge played 40 games per matchup, so its own 95% interval is about ±15 points; gaps under 20
points here are within noise of a single Forge run.
