Reading the table (inference from card lists, not tested):

- **Dimir Control vs Jund (51% vs Forge's 20%)** is still 31 points off even though Jund now has no
  ignored cards and Dimir Control only one. Card coverage no longer explains it; this looks like a
  pilot or rules difference, and the next step is to read a built-in log and a Forge log of this
  matchup.
- **Azorius Artifact Control** is the other large disagreement (vs Dimir Control and vs Izzet). It
  still ignores Braided Net and Perilous Snare, but it has few creatures, so how our AI pilots it
  (when to hold counters, how to use artifacts) is a likelier cause than the two cards.
- **Izzet vs Landfall (41% vs 72%)** has closed by 33 points. The leftover could be Landfall's two
  ignored cards (Demolition Field, Esper Origins) or piloting.
- **Boros Dragons** and **Dimir Midrange** still carry 3 and 6 ignored cards. They don't cause a
  flagged disagreement now, but are the next cards to support if their matchups get measured.
