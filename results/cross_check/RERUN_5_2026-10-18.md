## Why Dimir Control vs Jund is far from Forge

I ran this matchup in Forge here (Forge built from source with `scripts/install_forge.sh`): Dimir
Control won 18 of 112 games (16%; 7 of 40 in the run itself), Forge's first run had 8 of 40. The
built-in engine gives 51% to 57% in 300 games. I compared cards cast per game in the Forge logs with
100 built-in games (`compare_dimir_jund_logs.py`).

1. **Forge's AI does not play two of Dimir Control's cards.** Seasoned Cryomancer (4 copies) was cast
   0 times in 112 Forge games; it only appears being discarded or milled. Uldaros Theorix (2 copies)
   was cast 0 times. The built-in AI casts Cryomancer 1.1 times and Theorix 0.27 times per game.
   Replacing the four Cryomancers with an uncastable card in the built-in deck drops Dimir Control
   from 52% to 37% (300 games, noise about ±6); replacing Theorix too gives 43%. So about 10 to 15
   of the 30+ points come from Forge's pilot, not from rules.
2. **Jund plays its expensive sacrifice payoffs in Forge, much less often here.** Per game, Forge vs
   built-in: Lively Dirge 0.92 vs 0.49, Smaug the Magnificent 0.44 vs 0.14, Fanatical Offering 0.33 vs
   0.07, Mutagen Man 0.31 vs 0.18. Built-in Jund also has fewer lands on average (5.6 vs 6.3) and the
   games end sooner when Jund wins (13.6 vs 16.2 turns). I have not found why the built-in AI casts
   them less (spree modes for Dirge, sacrifice fodder for Offering are the first things to check in
   `ai.py`).
3. Both engines agree on the rest: Dimir Control's other cards (Deceit, Theorix Charm, Requiting Hex,
   Duress, Bitter Triumph, Overlord) are cast at similar rates, and the lands per game are close.

So no card or rules gap explains the difference. About a third to a half is Forge's AI not playing
Dimir Control's Cryomancer and Theorix; the rest looks like built-in Jund under-casting its
payoffs, which is an AI question and would change other Jund matchups too.
