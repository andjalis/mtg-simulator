## What still blocks the four flagged matchups

The ignored cards per deck are unchanged from the last round, so the blockers are the same:

- **Izzet Spellementals** (4 ignored: Flow State, Get Out, Hearth Elemental, Sunderflock). Three of
  these are the deck's engine: Hearth Elemental and Sunderflock are cost reductions scaled by a
  count (graveyard instants/sorceries/adventures; greatest Elemental mana value), Sunderflock also
  bounces all non-Elementals, and Get Out's two modes (counter a creature or enchantment spell,
  return up to two of your permanents) are ignored. Without them the deck is much weaker in the
  built-in engine. This drives both Izzet matchups (8% vs Landfall where Forge has 72%, and 97% for
  Boros Dragons where Forge has 57%).
- **Mono-Green Landfall** (2 ignored: Demolition Field, Esper Origins). Small effect; the deck
  isn't what's wrong in the Izzet matchup.
- **Boros Dragons** (3 ignored: Magda the Hoardmaster, Momo Friendly Flier, Sarkhan Dragon
  Ascendant). Magda's crime trigger and Treasure-to-Dragon ability, Momo's flying cost reduction,
  and Sarkhan's behold/Treasure. These are what make the deck stronger in Forge than here, but its
  matchups against Izzet go the other way, so Izzet's missing cards dominate.
- **Dimir Control** (3 ignored: Deadly Cover-Up, The Theorist Jace Beleren, Uldaros Theorix).
  Cover-Up's evidence mode and graveyard exile, the Jace's draw-step trigger and bounce, and
  Uldaros Theorix's free casting. Against Jund (52% vs Forge 20%) the missing card advantage and
  removal explain a lot of the deck's gap.
- **Jund Sacrifice** (4 ignored: Azog Moria's Ruin, Lively Dirge, Obsessive Pursuit, The
  Sackville-Bagginses). Mostly sacrifice payoffs and amass, which the deck needs to win.
- **Azorius Artifact Control** (7 ignored: Braided Net, Perilous Snare, Petrified Hamlet, Pinnacle
  Starcage, Repurposing Bay, The Irencrag, The Mind Stone). It still has almost no board presence
  of its own; against Dimir Control (35% vs 72%) it loses the long game.

The three matchups that now agree with Forge best are Azorius Artifact Control against Izzet, Landfall
and (within noise) Dimir Midrange and Jund: its ignored cards are mostly non-damage utility, so the
rest of its deck decides those games.

## Where to go next

Four cards would likely close the two biggest gaps, both Izzet matchups: Hearth Elemental and
Sunderflock (count-based cost reduction, with Sunderflock's mass bounce) and Get Out (counter and
return modes). After that, Dimir Control's three cards and Jund's sacrifice payoffs. The coverage
work that landed (copy effects, auras, equipment, cycling) mostly benefits decks outside this set.
